How it works

  1. Create a game and read out the 4-letter code. Everyone opens this site and types it in.
  2. Put it on a TV (optional) at /CODE/tv — that becomes the shared stage, and phones turn into controllers.
  3. One player is the judge each round. They pick a prompt from three.
  4. Everyone else answers with the funniest GIF from their hand of 7.
  5. The judge flips the answers one at a time, then crowns a winner. First to the target score wins.

The host sets judge rotation, points to win and timers in the lobby before starting. Works in one room or over a call.
